## ATLAS (atlas-ai.club)

- **What it is:** an AI team for cash-based clinics (PT, chiro). It is *not a chatbot, it's an org chart*. ATLAS is the CEO /
  orchestrator: you ask once, and it routes the work to specialist agents wired into your tools and numbers.
- **Core agents (free plan):** ATLAS (CEO, orchestrator), ARISTOTLE (marketing), DELPHI (data and analytics: weekly
  scorecard every Monday at 7am), IRIS (operations: daily briefing), MERCURY (sales: pipeline audits).
- **Pro specialists:** MINERVA (research), CLIO (data processing), PROMETHEUS (AI systems), with more every month.
- **How it works:** Chat, Hire, Delegate, Ground. Chat with the whole team; hire specialists from the marketplace;
  delegate recurring work (scorecards, briefings, reports); ground every answer in the clinic's own data and SOPs
  (Knowledge Base, with sources cited).
- **Integrations:** GoHighLevel, Meta Ads, Google Ads, HubSpot, Stripe, Google Sheets, Slack, Instagram, Google Calendar.
- **Offer:** beta, live now, with the first cohort closing soon. Free trial included. $297/month, or $2,970/year
  (≈ $247.50/mo, save 2 months). The beta unlocks everything: 5 core agents, 10+ Pro specialists, 2 million tokens,
  recurring tasks and all integrations.
- **CTA:** "DM us 'ATLAS' for early access", or app.atlas-ai.club. A spoken "click below" or "comment the word ATLAS"
  gets the CTA button.
- **Founders:** Dr. Odell Miller and Dr. Abby Sirovica, who have coached hundreds of clinics. "ATLAS is what we wish we had."
- **Voice:** short declarative lines. "Your clinic doesn't need another tool. It needs a team." "Read less. Decide
  faster." "Spend less time managing. More time treating." "Built for clinics. By clinicians."
- **Spelling:** ATLAS (all caps as a name on screen, "Atlas" is fine in speech), ChatGPT (never "chat GBT").
- **Brand:** orange #FF6B2C, black, white. The logo is the orange asterisk-over-bars mark.
