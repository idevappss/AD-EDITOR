Condensed from the full bank in shared/ODELL_STORY_BANK.md (v1–v6, Sep 22–28 2026): public podcasts, his YouTube vlogs and podcast tab, his Instagram captions, internal HBA recordings where he is the speaker, athletics records and platform bios. Only his own lines are kept; host, guest, client and member lines are excluded.

## 1. Timeline
- 1996 born Kalamazoo MI, raised Battle Creek. Gull Lake HS, three-sport athlete, First-Team All-SMAC linebacker, BCAM All-State basketball. Wanted college basketball; "too small at 6'4"".
- 2014 walked on at Western Michigan under P.J. Fleck as a D-end after a highlight tape made a week before signing day. Redshirted.
- 2015 moved to tight end ("too slow"), special teams, no scholarship. Walk-ons didn't eat with the team unless travelling.
- 2016 earned a full scholarship in fall camp, played all 14 games, Academic All-MAC. 2017–18 Academic All-MAC; senior year started 11 of 13 at TE. Won a championship at WMU.
- Apr 2019 Cleveland Browns mini camp as an undrafted free agent (moved to O-line, ~305 lb); also tried out for Washington. "Even when I was in the NFL, if my career lasted 10 years, I was going to start my own businesses."
- 2019–20 Uber, a scooter company with his own app (met the mayor; killed by compliance/COVID), Amazon, dropshipping, wedding DJ/videography started with a scholarship check.
- 2020–23 DPT at Midwestern University. Got shredded from ~305 to ~235 posting workouts; people asked for programs, so he sold the list, *then* built the Off The Bench app. Charged $80/month, nobody got results; a coach told him to charge more; $500/month or $2.5–3K packages for the same program and clients got results. $10–12K/month as a student, $20K/month online before the clinic existed. Up at 4 a.m. for ~3 years.
- Late 2022 car totalled; lease ending; single, so he moved into a VW van (written off through the DJ business). Parked behind the clinic or in a Planet Fitness lot; laundromat in Chicago; police knocked at 4 a.m. about a six-month overnight-parking ban he'd never noticed.
- Apr/May 2023 graduated. Five weeks solo in the van in San Diego, walking the beach: "Why not me?" Paid $20K+ for a business mentor as a new grad.
- Aug 2023 joined Aaron LeBauer's mastermind *before* opening. Fall 2023 saw patients with boxes still on the floor.
- Jan 2024 Off The Bench PT & Sports Performance opens (Kalamazoo/Portage). Sold $5K packages as a new grad. First full month $33K; paid ~4 years of rent in the first couple of months; months 1–2 ended the van and bought his first rental property. $50–51K months within six months, all organic.
- 2024 meets Abby (she DM'd; he didn't reply for 3–4 weeks; a mutual restaurant-owner friend connected them on business). Now married.
- Nov 2024 Dr. Nick Miller becomes Clinic Director (left a job with benefits to bet on a new grad). Odell later sold OTB; Dr. Nick takes patients from the consult.
- Jan 28 2026 book *The Ultimate Cash Based Practice* (Kindle, 208 pp). Tagline: "Playing it safe is the most dangerous thing you could do."
- 2026 Miami, 50th floor ($8K/month apartment). HBA HQ "aka the ninth floor of our apartment complex"; Miami office planned. Flight training in Fort Lauderdale, boxing lessons, incline walks while working. Closing on an apartment complex in Michigan; goal $10M in real estate by end of 2027. $600K, $700–860K months; "crazy faith" target of $1M/month within 12 months. Sep 23 2026 Atlas (HBA's AI agent) launched on the weekly coaching call. Sep 27 2026 back at his old WMU field in Kalamazoo.

**Numbers:** public bios disagree (Skool $12.4K student → $51K; Instagram $346k/month; TikTok $650k+/month; YouTube "$1M Physical Therapy business owner"); 2026 vlogs say $600K, $700–850K, $860K months. Use the number the video itself says, and never mix two in one piece.

## 2. Story bank
- **The walk-on.** Nobody D1 wanted him. "I'm actually glad I was a walk-on because I earned my scholarship." Earning it beats being handed it.
- **The NFL removed his fear.** Browns locker room with Baker Mayfield; after the 0.01% thing nothing in business scared him. Classmates cried before PT practicals; he'd "played in front of a hundred thousand people." Not making a 10-year career: "is that a failure, or did I learn a lot of things that are going to take me well beyond where I ever thought I could?"
- **The mission.** Got D1 and NFL-level care, saw everyday people don't: "bring NFL and high-level care to everyday individuals." HBA mission: "indirectly serve 1 million patients a year by empowering physical therapists and chiropractors to build thriving businesses."
- **Entrepreneur before doctor.** "I'm an entrepreneur turned doctor of physical therapy." Uber after winning games. The failed scooter company taught what got OTB to a $51K month. His father: "You have so many pans in the fire."
- **The pricing lesson.** $80 → $500, same program, better results. "People pay for what they value." "I would rather see 15 patients a week at $500 each than 50 at $80. I'm going to get more no's, but I'm also going to get my time back."
- **The app that paid for school.** "My audience decided what they wanted." Sell it, then build it.
- **The weird kid.** "While my classmates were going out on the weekend celebrating that they just finished an exam… I was setting the foundation for my life." "2 and a half years later, the gap is evident." Nothing was overnight.
- **The van.** Not hardship: cut the expense, keep the vision, reinvest. "Everything happens for you, not to you." "I want to keep everything minimalist."
- **Paying for speed.** $20K mentor as a new grad → $50K/month in under six months: "I'm not smart enough… I didn't do it on my own." "I'd rather pay for speed. Every single time." Has nutrition, business and relationship coaches. $50K organic "wasn't a flex" — knowing what he knows now it would have been $150–200K.
- **Environment.** At $20K/month as a new grad "you start to think you're big dog, and then you hit a ceiling." "Your goals should be the norm in the room." In his mastermind rooms "we're bottom of the pack. It forces us to grow."
- **Still scared to invest.** "I have the money 20 times over and it's still nerve-wracking." Time is finite, money is abundant: "Spend the abundant resource to save the finite." "It's an investment, not an expense."
- **Skills over money.** "If I lost a million dollars today, I actually have the ability to go tomorrow and earn that million dollars back." Spoke 45 minutes at a workshop, walked with $20K.
- **Meeting Abby.** She DM'd, he was heads-down for weeks; she owned two chiro offices, a restaurant, real estate. "Marry someone just as OBSESSED and PASSIONATE about achieving their goals as you are yours."
- **Documenting, not creating.** "I don't create content. I document my life." Filmed himself studying in PT school; seven months of footage banked; Google folders by patient/session for his editor.
- **Anti-standardization / who made that up?** Asked "why do we do it this way" in PT school; professors often didn't know. "Somebody along the way made that up, and every healthcare professional was following it… Set your own rules."
- **Grow mode.** "Even at 700 or 850K, we're still in grow mode… investing back into team big time." Coast mode is $1.5–2M/month. "Hold your breath the longest" — don't spend on luxury, fund the business. "You get to the top of the mountain and you just see the next one."
- **Thinking small is selfish.** "One of the most selfish things you can do is to think too small." "My basic needs are met. If I was only thinking about my needs, I would have stopped a long time ago." Wants to retire his parents.
- **Money is anticlimactic.** His first $10K month: "I don't feel any different than yesterday." The million-dollar month will be too, so purpose has to come first.
- **Money vs. mission / faith.** Clinicians are taught business is dirty and patients pay for it. "I believe in Jesus. I just want to be a light." "I serve an abundant God." "Not always chasing money, but the more money I can have, the more I can impact others."
- **Leading, not managing.** "I'm not a manager. I'm a true entrepreneur." The Implementation Ladder (from Myron Golden): implementation → managing → communication → imagination. "I'm doing the world a disservice if I'm seeing all the patients." "I used to hate speaking, then something switched." Delegation "is like unlocking my brain." Letting a team member go "is never easy."
- **Hiring.** Hire and fire on core values (crazy faith, speed, overcommunication, growth mindset, overdelivery). Group interview, a week of shadowing: "I want to see how they talk to the receptionist." Asks students "how were you making money in grad school." Hire order: admin buys back delivery, delivery buys back sales, then marketing, then leadership. "A new grad is the best opportunity — no bad habits." "A small lethal team."
- **We compress time.** "Instead of 6 months, we try to compress that into 2 weeks. A year into a month. 10 years into a year."
- **AI / Atlas.** "Faster horses." Most people use "2, 3, 5% of what AI can actually do." "When you speak with AI, AI then feeds you back more work to do" — agents do the work. "All we are is just walking loops… anything a human can do that's looping, AI can do." "AI's not meant to replace your team… it's going to allow one person to do the role of ten." "The only limitation that we have in 2026 is our imagination."
- **Coaching.** "Our job is not to be your friend." "10 years to teach the next person how to do it in a week. That's coaching." "Look under the hood": give him your key numbers and in 10 minutes he'll name the bottleneck. "Business is inherently very, very basic. You attract, you convert, you deliver." "There's a million ways to make $1 million."
- **Marketing.** "The audience that doesn't know you is 100 times bigger than those who do." Of six channels, "you only need to really unlock one." Ads: make 15 in 30 minutes, 1 in 10 works, keep making more. Hooks come from the patient's life ("when they bend over to tie their shoe"). Not salesy: "we're not like 'hey, book a call' desperately." "A referral — they walk in pre-sold." "We're cashiers instead of trying to sell someone."
- **The mentor lens.** Learned business by osmosis — Gary V on Uber drives, podcasts on 4am workouts, Elon, Carnegie, *Buy Back Your Time*. Virtual mentors count.

How he talks: stacked repetition ("do it do it do it", "very, very basic"); short declaratives, then a "so" that lands the lesson; asks the room a question and answers it ("guess what I was doing?"); self-aware about his gaps ("I'm not smart enough"); gives the number, the month, the name; football framing (reps, game day, locker room, coach, "the arena that forged me"); calls his audience "Doc"; vocabulary: zealous, extremist, asset, osmosis, main thing, elevate, speed, document, impact, compress, leverage, lethal, abundant; faith stated plainly, never preached.

Written voice (Instagram): 1–3 short lines; 1–3 words in ALL CAPS for stress (COMPLEXITY, OBSESSED, DEBT, TEAM, ZEALOUS); a turn in the second line; often "It's also available to you."; emojis 😤🚀🙏🏼; 3–5 hashtags.

On a sales call: warm, fast, affirming, then a direct close — credit for showing up, names their traits ("that tells me you're an action taker"), freedom questions ("Tell me your dream week"), "How committed are you, out of 10? What's keeping you at an 8?"

What he is not: not a hype guy (certainty, not exclamation marks); not a victim narrator (hardship gets reframed: "glad I was a walk-on"); never vague; never desperate or salesy.
