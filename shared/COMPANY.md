It is run by husband-and-wife doctors **Dr. Odell Miller** (DPT, CEO) and **Dr. Abby Miller** (DC, CFO), based in **Miami**. The front door is a free workshop, and the business is documented in public on YouTube and Instagram as it pushes toward **$1M a month**.

## 3. Offers and funnel

- **Strategy call.** Booked from the workshop, and the strategy team closes it (about $15K paid in full is typical). Targets: show rate above 75%, close above 50%.

## 6. What we teach (the frameworks)
Name these exactly as Odell does. They make great titles, carousels and captions.

1. **The 6 numbers you must track:** cost per lead → booking % → show rate → close rate → cost per acquisition → AOV/ROAS → LTV. *"If I look at these numbers I can tell you what to do next without looking at your Instagram."* Above 3:1 ROAS is good. Close above 80% means **raise your price**.
2. **The $100K/month flywheel:** client success (results) → retention, LTV and reviews (video testimonials, text-message screenshots as "trust assets") → marketing (make clients the star) → sales (*"you're a cashier, not a car salesman"*, "lay-down deal") → back to client success.
3. **Big head, long tail:** a higher front-end price while they're in pain ($3K+), then a cheap wellness membership ($200 a month). It raises LTV.
4. **Increasing LTV:** big head long tail, NPS above 9 (cleanliness, booking experience, provider, likelihood to refer), better table talk (*"they couldn't care less about your weekend"*), and a clear patient roadmap from week 1 to the wellness program.
5. **Six marketing channels:** VSL funnel, workshop, internal referral, external referral, DM funnel, application funnel. *"Paid is just fuel to the fire."* **Maximize one channel before moving to the next.** *"There's a million ways to make a million dollars."* *"You only need to unlock one."*
6. **VSL math:** 100 people to the page gives about 4 clients, and at $3K that's $12K a day. *"Your only job is getting 100 people to that page."*
7. **External referrals:** find someone who already has authority with your ideal client, and market to them as if they were a client.
8. **Post-purchase sequence:** automated testimonials, video reviews, referrals and NPS after someone buys.
9. **Buy back your time, in this order:** admin ("if you don't have an assistant, you are the assistant") → delivery (*"delivering care is one of the lowest-value tasks"*) → sales → **marketing last** (the engine) → leadership (the true cap).
10. **Who not how** (the book). *"If you have money, you no longer have a problem."* *"80% done by someone else is 100% better than done by you."*
11. **Leadership:** build leaders, not followers. **Freedom is directly related to the release of control.** Give away the credit. *"Your capacity is how much stress you can hold."* Leaders are firefighters (Gary V). Jethro and Moses: appoint leaders. Hire transfers, not freshmen. No participation trophies. Hire and fire on core values, and repeat mission, vision and values every meeting.
12. **Think exponentially, not linearly.** *"You only have to win once."*
13. **Studying a mentor:** copy their **mental syntax** (how they think), **philosophy** (how they see the world) and **physiology** (how they carry themselves, speak and dress).
14. **Exposure:** *"You don't know you're in a box."* Get in rooms where you're the smallest.
15. **Sales psychology:** speak with authority, *"We're not meant to be your friends, we're meant to call you higher."* Collect "answers to the test" early. Pull calls forward. Keep following up until they say stop. Vet them as much as they vet you.
16. **Turn yourself into a media company**, in your own town. *"Even with a thousand followers you can monetize it."*

**From the "Collapse the Timeline" mastermind keynote** (tagline *"Scale the revenue. Scale the freedom. Design the business around your life."*):

17. **Business is three jobs: attract** (marketing) **→ convert** (sales) **→ deliver** (client success). *"We make it complicated. It was never complicated."*
18. **The million-dollar formula** (after Myron Golden): take something complex, simplify it, then multiply the simplification. *"Simplify first. Multiply second."*
19. **The Exponential Growth Algorithm** (see HBA 2.0). *"If you haven't eliminated at least 10%, you haven't looked hard enough."* *"Automation amplifies whatever you give it — good and bad."*
20. **10X is easier than 2X.** 2X is a grind; 10X forces you to delete 80% of what you do. *"10X is a decision about what you stop doing."*
21. **Collapse-the-timeline exercise:** write the 10-year goal, now do it in 3 years, now in 1.
22. **The Identity Ceiling:** *"The person who got you here won't be the person who gets you there."* **You 2.0:** draw the next version of yourself.
23. **The rocket / law of the booster:** every stage has an expiration date. *"Every time you let go of something, you grow."* **The art of letting go** covers people, ideas, identity and systems. *"The CEO at $30K/month and the CEO at $200K/month are not the same person."*
24. **Covey's big rocks first.**
25. **Four levels of work:** implementation → management → communication → **imagination** ("where endless wealth is built").
26. **Speed:** 51% conviction is enough, 1 in 10 wins, and decision speed is the #1 trait. *"The cost isn't being wrong. It's the time you waste not deciding."*
27. **The kinked hose:** find and obliterate bottlenecks.
28. **High hope:** the goal is unwavering, the path is flexible. *"Busy is not progress."* *"Five years is twenty quarters."*

**Recurring coaching lines** (Sept 23 call): be the **chief repeating officer** (repeat the 1–3 things that matter); *"our job is not to be your friend"*; reversible decisions deserve no deliberation; the people ahead aren't better, they're **willing to fail faster**; hire fast, fire faster; **question requirements** (*"somebody along the way just made that rule up"*, e.g. letting patients pay later: *"when did you last go to a steak dinner and ask to pay two months later?"*, so use cards on file and same-day payment); a new hire buys back **delivery first**, and an eval's job is to see fit, not to treat; AI doesn't replace your team, it lets one person do the work of ten (*"we care about results, not your time"*).

**The ads framework HBA teaches:** keep it simple. One campaign with broad + lookalike (and warm, if you have a big audience) ad sets, with the same ads in each. Make 10–15 ads: problem-aware, product-aware and solution-aware hooks, just speak (you can make 30 in an hour). **1 in 10 works.** Refilm every 2–3 weeks, and replace ads at least every 30 days. Track only the 7–8 funnel numbers. If ROAS is high, spend more, raising budgets 30% or less at a time.

## 8. Content engine (how HBA makes content)
- **Daily vlogs:** "a day in the life of a multi-million-dollar CEO", "a week in our life growing an 8-figure business". Real meetings, strategy calls, gym, macros, flight lessons, Miami, real estate. *"Things a lot of people won't see — that's why I document my entire life."*

- **Clipping pages:** editors post clips across many pages to become "a media company". The push is toward **more AI, less headcount** in editing (the reason this app exists).
